Chapter 01 • Official Mission Call (Ref: 2246341)

The Official Missionary Call

Office of the First Presidency
Assigned Field Dumaguete Mission Negros Oriental
Language Cebuano Native Tongue
MTC Entrance Dec 11, 2026 Report Date: Friday
Term of Service 24 Months Ambassador of Christ
The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints • Salt Lake City, Utah

Dear Elder Salviejo,

You are hereby called to serve as a missionary of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. You are assigned to labor in the Philippines Dumaguete Mission. You should report to the Missionary Training Center on Friday, December 11, 2026. You will prepare to preach the gospel in the Cebuano language.

Your term of service will be for 24 months. You have been recommended as one worthy to represent the Lord as an ambassador of the Savior Jesus Christ. An eternal work awaits you as you share the message of the restored gospel.

Faithfully yours, The First Presidency
