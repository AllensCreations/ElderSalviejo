Term of Service 24 Months Full-Time Minister
“At first, wala akong desire na mag-mission. Pero in my darkest moments, binigyan ako ni Heavenly Father ng liwanag na hindi ko inakala na kailangan ko.”

Through the love ng YSA district ko, sa spirit ng YSA Conferences, at sa pagserve ko as an FSY Counselor, nasoften ni Heavenly Father yung puso ko at tinawag Niya ako sa Kanyang gawain.

Ako ay gitawag isip usa ka full-time missionary sa The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints sa Philippines Dumaguete Mission! 🇵🇭

Ug musolod na ko sa MTC sa December 11, 2026.

Sacred Promise
Moroni 10:5
“By the power of the Holy Ghost ye may know the truth of all things.”

I don’t know everything that’s ahead, and I have fears... especially about teaching. But I hold to this promise: 'by the power of the Holy Ghost ye may know the truth of all things' - Moroni 10:5.

Alam ko na totoo ang Atonement ni Jesus Christ, at Siya yung reason kung bakit may hope at faith ako to go.

Such a huge privilege to be an instrument ng Panginoon to share His Gospel at ang love ni Jesus Christ sa mga tao sa Dumaguete Mission.

Sobrang thankful ako sa lahat ng support, guidance, at prayers ng family ko, friends, at leaders na laging nandiyan.

I go with a grateful heart to serve, to love, at para maging instrument sa mga kamay ng Panginoon. Please keep me in your prayers!

Here am I, send me.
Elder Mark Salviejo
